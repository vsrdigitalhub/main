Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a member of VSR Digital Hub?
Click "Join Hub", fill in your details, and your account is created instantly — no approval wait.
Where can I track my project requests?
Every request you raise appears on your customer dashboard along with its current status.
What services does VSR Digital Hub offer?
Web development, UI/UX design, digital marketing, video editing, branding, and freelance community support — see the full list on our Services page.
Is my account information secure?
Yes. Passwords are hashed and all account data is stored in a secured MySQL database, never in plain text.
How much does a project cost?
It depends on scope — check our Pricing page for starting packages, or contact us for a custom quote.
Can I request more than one service at a time?
Yes — raise as many requests as you like from your dashboard, and each one is tracked separately.
How do I get in touch with the team?
Use the Contact page, email us directly, or raise a request from your dashboard if you're already a member.
